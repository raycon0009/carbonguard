Kariba is not an outlier

Carbon markets only work if one credit is one real tonne, counted once. Published research says neither holds reliably.

Verification is slow, manual and opaque. Buyers can't tell good credits from bad ones, and the same tonne can be tokenised, sold or claimed twice.

  • ~94%of credits from sampled rainforest projects did not reflect real reductions (West et al., Science, 2023)
  • ~9×over-crediting estimated for cookstove projects (Gill-Wiehl et al., Nature Sustainability, 2024)
  • <16%of credits assessed represent real emission reductions (Probst et al., Nature Communications, 2024)
  • 2022crypto bridges tokenised millions of old registry credits; Verra banned tokenising retired credits over double counting
