Can you trust the AI?

Scores are statistical risk indicators from public data, not allegations. Here is how the frozen model does on real projects, including where it fails.

Where it fails

AUC 0.56

No better than chance on 65 California forest offsets graded against Badgley et al. (2021). Their over-crediting came from how baseline carbon stocks were set, which leaves no trace in registry data. Like Kariba before satellite data, it needs outside evidence: forest-inventory data. Study

No hidden regional bias

Country is never a model input. Each region's High rate is compared with what its mix of project types alone predicts.

The model

Weak-supervised gradient boosting plus an isolation-forest anomaly detector, trained on 420 synthetic and 6,038 real projects. The 69 real projects used to judge it were held out.

What drives a score

Provenance on every score

Every score on chain carries these hashes, so anyone can check which model judged which credit. The AI can only freeze; unfreezing takes 2 of 3 independent human verifiers.
