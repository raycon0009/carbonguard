Limits

A screening estimate, not an audit. A distance ring is a simpler control than the synthetic-control matching in published studies, the satellite product can miss slow degradation of dry woodland, and boundaries are as published. There is no imagery basemap: land and water come from the satellite data mask, and each map cell covers many 30 m pixels.
