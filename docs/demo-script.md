# Demo video script (3:00)

Record at 1080p with the browser zoomed to 110%. Before recording, reset the chain so the data is clean: run `npm run chain`, then `npm run demo:local`.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:20** · Problem | Title slide, then headlines (Guardian 2023 "phantom credits", Verra's 2022 tokenisation ban) | "Carbon credits are supposed to mean one tonne of CO₂, counted once. Research in *Science* and *Nature* found that most credits checked don't deliver what they claim, and the same tonne can be sold twice, tokenised twice or claimed by two parties. When buyers can't trust credits, money stops flowing to real climate projects." |
| **0:20–0:35** · Idea | Dashboard hero and KPIs | "CarbonGuard puts an AI quality gate in front of the market and enforces 'counted once' in a smart contract. Here, 7.6 million tonnes, over half of this supply, are kept away from buyers as high-risk." |
| **0:35–1:15** · AI | Scroll to the table and click the top REDD+ row to open the drawer | "Each batch is scored by our model, weak-supervised gradient boosting plus an anomaly detector. It doesn't just give a number, it explains itself: this REDD+ project issued 5.9 times its own estimate, and satellite data shows 78% less avoided deforestation than it claims. The score, the reasons and the hashes of the exact model and data are written on chain, so anyone can audit which AI judged which credit." Scroll to the Real-world check card: "We measured real satellite data for real projects. Kariba, where Verra itself found 15 million excess credits, looks clean on registry data, scoring 6. Satellites show 99% of its credits aren't backed by avoided deforestation, and the score jumps to 64. Two other REDD+ projects get frozen. And we tell you where we fail: on California forest offsets, we're no better than chance yet." |
| **1:15–2:00** · Blockchain guards | Click the three attack buttons one at a time | "Let's try to cheat. One: re-bridge serials that are already on chain, which is the exact failure that made Verra ban crypto bridges. The contract blocks it and names the overlapping batch. Two: sell credits the AI flagged. They're frozen. Three: use a credit for an airline's CORSIA claim when the host country never applied an Article 6 corresponding adjustment. That's blocked too, so there's no double claiming." |
| **2:00–2:30** · Human in the loop and retirement | Connect the wallet, Review a flagged batch and Approve it, then Retire 1,000 t for "Acme Corp" and open the certificate | "AI flags and humans decide. A verifier's decision is stored on chain with an audit note. Now a company retires clean credits. They're burned, so they can never be resold, and here's the public certificate, rendered live from the blockchain with the risk score and model hash at the moment of retirement." |
| **2:30–3:00** · Impact and scale | Slide: who uses it and the roadmap | "Registries can run CarbonGuard before tokenising, buyers can screen credits before purchase, and national Article 6 registries can enforce corresponding adjustments. Next we extend satellite measurement to every forest project with a published boundary, add forest-inventory data, move the oracle to Chainlink Functions, and deploy on an L2. CarbonGuard: every tonne scored by AI, every tonne counted once." |

## Tips
- Rehearse once with a timer, and cut words rather than speeding up.
- Keep the cursor still while you talk, and zoom in (Ctrl + scroll) on the reasons column.
- If MetaMask is slow, record the wallet part separately and edit it in.
